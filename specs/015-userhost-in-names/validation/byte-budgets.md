# Byte-budget evidence

Actual discovered descriptors reserve status P=2 and identity I=92, even while disabled. For S=16, N=9, the standalone NAMES layout reaches 512 at C=371 and 513 at C=372. 366 reaches 512 at C=458 and 513 at C=459. These layout checks isolate the NAMES formulas; they do not claim these configurations satisfy every other reply.

The independent WHO bound reaches 512 at C=350, then rejects C=351, with both providers installed. WHO minus expanded NAMES is S+5 bytes, so installation raises the NAMES reservation without newly rejecting any configuration already accepted by current WHO validation. This corrects the planning prediction of a configuration compatibility tightening.

NamesByteBudgetTest uses real discovered providers and MemberReplies with 12 nine-byte nicknames, 27-byte UTF-8 idents and 63-byte multibyte display hosts; the 350-byte channel and nine-byte requester force complete tokens onto separate bounded lines. Exact member content and no duplicates/loss plus final 366 text are asserted. The socket walkthrough additionally proves two-line packing with a multibyte channel and idents.
